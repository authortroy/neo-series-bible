# NEO Series Bible

**NEO, Hugh Howey's word processor for authors, with The Plot Dude's Series Bible built in.**

NEO Series Bible is an unofficial edition of [NEO](https://github.com/hughhowey/neo). Everything that makes NEO what it is (the bookshelf, the blank page, Enter-Enter-Enter) is Hugh Howey's work, free and open source, and all of it is here unchanged. This edition adds one thing: your series bible, inside the book you're writing.

It is a free companion to [The Plot Dude's Series Bible](https://theplotabides.com/tools/series-bible-updater/) app. It is made by Troy Lambert (The Plot Dude), not by Hugh.

## What it adds

- **Characters, Locations and Events tabs** on any book the Series Bible app has scanned. View only, searchable with Find.
- **An Ask tab.** Search the bible on any computer, offline. Ask a question about the open book and the Series Bible app answers it.
- **"Series Bible Summary of What You Wrote"** on each chapter's outline card, under your own note. Your note is never changed.

NEO never edits the bible. The Series Bible app builds it; NEO shows it.

## What you need

| | |
|---|---|
| **To see the tabs and summaries** | The Series Bible app, 1.3.0 or later, on a computer that has scanned the book |
| **To ask questions** | The Series Bible app, 1.4.0 or later, open on the same computer |
| **To see "Also known as" names** | The Series Bible app, 1.5.0 or later |

Without the Series Bible app, NEO Series Bible is simply NEO.

## Download

The easiest way is the download page at **[theplotabides.com/tools/neo-series-bible](https://theplotabides.com/tools/neo-series-bible/)**, which also has the install guide and a single download with both apps.

Every release is also on the [Releases page](../../releases/latest):

- **Mac:** the `arm64` `.dmg` for Apple Silicon (M1 and newer), or the `x64` `.dmg` for older Intel Macs. Signed and notarized by Apple. Open it and drag NEO Series Bible to Applications.
- **Windows (test edition):** `NEO-Series-Bible-Setup`. It is not signed, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.

## If you already use NEO

- NEO Series Bible opens **the same library** as NEO. Your books, shelves and settings are all there, and nothing about them is changed.
- NEO and NEO Series Bible are separate apps and can both be installed. They won't open the library at the same time: quit one before opening the other.
- You can go back to plain NEO whenever you like.

## Updates

NEO Series Bible updates itself, from this page's releases. When Hugh releases a new NEO, a new NEO Series Bible built on it follows, usually the same day.

The Series Bible app updates itself too, separately. Each app tells you when the other one has an update waiting, so the two stay current together.

Please don't send Series Bible questions to Hugh. He didn't build this part and can't fix it. Inside the app, the Help menu has the same two sections.
